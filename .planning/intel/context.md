# Ingested Product Context

Source: `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`

## Problem Statement
Students living away from home face persistent everyday logistical frictions:
1. High individual delivery fees and surging minimum order thresholds on quick-commerce apps (Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart) when ordering late-night snacks or basic groceries.
2. Expensive single-rider taxi and auto fares to transport terminals (airports, railway stations) and university campuses.
3. Lack of verified, privacy-safe building-level networks for residents living in the same hostel or PG.

## Value Proposition
- **Split & Save:** Eliminates duplicate delivery fees and splits cab fares.
- **Physical Living Focus:** Organizes around actual living residences rather than abstract campus rosters.
- **Privacy First:** Verified physical pickup points and masked handles ensure student safety.
